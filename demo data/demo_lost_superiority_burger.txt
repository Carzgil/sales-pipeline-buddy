This call is a clean structural non-fit loss. The rep was not outperformed

   operation. There is no commission to cut because there is no delivery

   integration, no DoorDash or Uber Eats badge, and no ordering CTA of
   any kind. The website is informational only. This is a pre-call
   disqualifier, not a mid-call discovery.

all. A pre-call brief that checks for online ordering infrastructure --
not just third-party presence -- would have flagged this before the rep
dialed. No ordering infrastructure plus counter service model equals
no fit.
