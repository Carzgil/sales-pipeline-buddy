WHY THIS CALL WAS LOST — COACHING NOTES
================================================================================

This was a warm re-engagement call to a prospect who had submitted a grader
report in December but never heard back. The rep had real SEO data and used
it in the conversation, which is a strength. However, several avoidable gaps
prevented the booking.

1. WRONG WEBSITE REFERENCED IN PITCH: Carolina's Mexican Food has a clear
   brand identity and its own domain. Mid-call, the rep referenced the website
   as "cheeseandstuffedeli.com," which is an entirely different restaurant.
   This was likely a data mix-up and immediately undermined the rep's claim
   of having done specific research on this restaurant.

2. NO KNOWLEDGE OF THE PROSPECT'S CURRENT SOLUTION: The prospect revealed they
   already had a web person working on their site. A simple check of the
   restaurant's website before the call would have shown the current state of
   their web presence and whether a web team was already active. The rep had no
   response to this objection and was caught flat-footed.

3. CORRECT CUISINE NOT CONFIRMED: Carolina's Mexican Food is a landmark Phoenix
   Mexican food restaurant, famous in the local market. The rep referenced
   menu items like "cactus jack" and "grilled cheese" as generic examples,
   which are not relevant to a Mexican fast-casual restaurant. Knowing the
   menu before calling allows for more resonant examples.

4. SOFT CLOSE THEN GAVE UP TOO EASILY: The prospect said they were busy and
   wanted to push things down the road. The rep accepted this almost
   immediately, promised to call back "next Friday," and ended the call with
   no committed meeting. The rep did not connect the SEO opportunity they had
   clearly articulated to an urgent reason to act.

5. APOLOGY FOR DELAYED OUTREACH: The rep apologized for no one reaching out
   since December. While honest, this acknowledged an organizational failure
   without offering any value to compensate for it, further weakening the rep's
   position.

KEY LESSON: Confirming you have the right website and the right menu on screen
before referencing either is non-negotiable. When a prospect says they have a
web person, the prepared rep has a ready answer that acknowledges that and
explains how Owner.com is different and complementary.
